Topic 5.1: Physical Modelling Synthesis Architecture

Physical modeling makes sound by copying how a real object vibrates. Instead of a stored waveform, the synth calculates the physical behavior of a real object. Select physical properties below to observe how excitation, damping, and stiffness sculpt acoustic responses.

Physical Model: Struck String (High Damping, Low Stiffness)

The excitation is a sharp strike/pluck. High damping means the vibration loses energy quickly (like putting a hand on a bell). Low stiffness produces harmonic, musical string vibration.

Analogy: a plastic ruler. Hold a ruler over the table edge and flick the end. The flick is the excitation. The ruler is the resonator. Flick it harder and you get a louder, brighter sound.
Why this matters for MIDI & MPE: This is the family closest to real playing. Aftertouch can stand for bow pressure, the mod wheel for breath, and velocity for how hard a string or bar is struck. Standards like MPE (MIDI Polyphonic Expression, which gives each note its own pitch and pressure) suit these instruments especially well.
